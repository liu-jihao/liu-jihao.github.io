Relation to Écalle’s route. Écalle’s 1992 monograph proves Dulac’s theorem in two stages: formalization, which attaches to the return map of a polycycle a formal object (a transseries), and actualization, which shows that the formal object determines the actual map, so that a nonzero formal displacement forces a sign of the actual displacement near the polycycle. The actualization is carried out there through accelero-summation in the class of analysable functions, with median averages at the saddle-nodes. This manuscript keeps the two-stage architecture but changes the devices. There are no acceleration operators, no median or well-behaved averages, and no transseries class.
