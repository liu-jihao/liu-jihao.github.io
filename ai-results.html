3. Fujita freeness in dimension six
Jihao Liu
AI-generated paper · proof accepted by the Danus verifier 2026-09-09 08:56:09 UTC · posted here 2026-09-20 06:51:18 UTC · not yet submitted to arXiv

This paper proves Fujita’s base-point-freeness conjecture in dimension six: KX + 7L is globally generated for every smooth complex projective sixfold X and every ample Cartier divisor L. The conjecture was previously known in dimensions at most five, by Reider, Ein–Lazarsfeld, Kawamata, and Ye–Zhu. At each point, the proof constructs an effective rational boundary of coefficient strictly less than seven with that point as its minimal log canonical center. It combines bounds for complete cutting chains with uniform local dimension estimates and amplification of complete spaces of sections. Danus ran with seven workers; from the creation of the project to the acceptance of the theorem took about 47 hours of calendar time, including an interruption.

2. Nonhyperlinear groups exist
Jihao Liu
AI-generated paper · proof accepted by the Danus verifier 2026-09-19 22:29:37 UTC · posted here 2026-09-20 06:24:54 UTC · not yet submitted to arXiv

This paper constructs a countable discrete group that is not hyperlinear. Equivalently, its group von Neumann algebra, with its canonical trace, does not embed into an ultrapower of the hyperfinite II1 factor, so Connes’ embedding problem has a negative answer for groups. The group is the generalized wreath product W = (⊕G/Γ Z/2) ⋊ G, where Γ = EL3(F2[x1, x2, x3]) and G = EL3(F2[x1±1, x2±1, x3±1]) ⋊ SL3(Z). It is one of the groups that Kun and Thom (arXiv:2608.06222) proved to be nonsofic, building on the first nonsofic group, found by OpenAI.

The new ingredients are two theorems on tracial ultraproducts of matrix algebras. First, the relative commutant of a finitely generated Kazhdan group in such an ultraproduct is itself an ultraproduct of finite-dimensional subalgebras of the same matrix algebras; this answers Open Problem 6.2(a) of Alekseev and Thom (arXiv:2608.05362). Second, if a Kazhdan group is generated by a subgroup together with finitely many elements that conjugate this subgroup into itself, then the group normalizes the relative commutant of the subgroup. Danus ran with seven proof workers and found the proof in 7 hours and 11 minutes.
