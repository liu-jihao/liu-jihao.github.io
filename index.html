Algebraic geometer at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, and a member of the Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research (BICMR). Mostly thinking about birational geometry — the minimal model program, generalized pairs, foliations, singularities, Fano varieties and K-stability, boundedness — and how explicit one can make any of it. From time to time I also work on problems in other parts of mathematics.

Before returning to Beijing in 2024 I spent three years at Northwestern as a Boas Assistant Professor, working with Yuchen Liu. My Ph.D. (Utah, 2021) was supervised by Christopher Hacon; before that, my undergraduate years at PKU were spent under Gang Tian.
