Algebraic geometer at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, and a member of the Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research (BICMR). Mostly thinking about birational geometry — minimal models, singularities, foliations, Fano varieties and their K-stability — and how explicit one can make any of it. Lately also building and using AI systems for research mathematics.

Before returning to Beijing in 2024 I spent three years at Northwestern as a Boas Assistant Professor, working with Yuchen Liu. My Ph.D. (Utah, 2021) was supervised by Christopher Hacon; before that, my undergraduate years at PKU were spent under Gang Tian.

Research

Birational geometry · singularities · foliations · Fano varieties and K-stability · explicit birational geometry · AI for mathematics.

Most of my papers are about the minimal model program and the singularities it produces: flips and minimal models for generalized pairs (log canonical case) and for algebraically integrable foliations, and ACC-type questions for minimal log discrepancies and log canonical thresholds.

A second thread is Fano varieties and K-stability: explicit bounds for Fano threefolds, exceptional Fano varieties, ACC for local volumes (the local side of K-stability), alpha invariants of K-polystable toric Fano varieties, a Demailly-type identity on Fano manifolds, and a counterexample to the cscK Yau–Tian–Donaldson conjecture.

Since 2026 I also work on AI for mathematics. I am one of the developers of Danus, a system of reasoning agents for research-level problems, and I use it both inside algebraic geometry and outside it, for instance on the equality case of Ehrhart’s volume conjecture. Results obtained this way that no human has verified yet are kept on a separate page.
