Large local volumes of threefold singularities

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (177 pages)

Abstract: We affirmatively answer Questions 5.3 and 5.4 in Yuchen Liu’s paper on optimal bounds for local volumes of threefold singularities. We prove that, among the local volumes of klt threefold singularities, those that are at least 9 are exactly the ten values listed in Question 5.3. We also prove that, among klt threefold singularities that are not finite quasi-étale quotients of local complete intersection singularities, the vertex of the anti-canonical cone over the first Hirzebruch surface has the largest local volume, and that up to analytic isomorphism it is the only such singularity with the largest local volume.
