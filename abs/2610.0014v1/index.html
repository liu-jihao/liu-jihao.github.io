Non-isomorphic endomorphisms of Fano manifolds of Picard number one

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (22 pages)

Abstract: We prove that a Fano manifold of Picard number one admitting a surjective endomorphism of degree greater than one is isomorphic to projective space, which confirms a conjecture that Hwang and Nakayama describe as folklore since the 1980s. As a consequence, a smooth projective rationally connected variety of Picard number one admitting an int-amplified endomorphism is isomorphic to projective space, and in particular toric, which answers the Picard number one case of questions of Fakhruddin and of Meng and Zhong.
