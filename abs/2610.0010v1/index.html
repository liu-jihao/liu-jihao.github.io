K-semistability of smooth Fano threefolds of Picard number one

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. The author is collaborating with Hamid Abban, Paolo Cascini, Ivan Cheltsov, Masafumi Hattori, and Constantin Shramov for further understanding and polishing the writing of the paper.)

PDF (137 pages)

Abstract: We prove that every smooth complex Fano threefold of Picard number one is K-semistable. The remaining prime families have anticanonical degrees 18 and 22. We control their divisorial valuations by complete section refinements, using line projections and weighted point extractions, and treat the special members in each family. The main result of this paper was obtained using generative AI, particularly GPT-6 Astra and the Danus system.
