Pushforwards of adjoint bundles with nef twists need not be GV-sheaves

Jihao Liu

PDF (6 pages)

Abstract: We answer a question of Meng negatively: there exist a smooth morphism with connected fibers from a smooth projective fourfold to an abelian surface and a nef line bundle on the fourfold such that the pushforward of the canonical bundle of the fourfold twisted by the nef line bundle is not a GV-sheaf.
