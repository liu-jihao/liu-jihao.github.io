Classification of numerical Godeaux surfaces

Jihao Liu, Wenfei Liu, and Lei Zhang

PDF (204 pages)

Abstract: We prove Reid’s conjecture that the moduli space of numerical Godeaux surfaces has exactly five irreducible components, one for each cyclic fundamental group of order at most 5. In particular, there exist exactly five deformation classes of numerical Godeaux surfaces, and any numerical Godeaux surface with torsion-free Picard group is deformation equivalent to the Barlow surface.

Comments:204 pages. Proves the conjecture of Reid, which is Conjecture 1 of [Bauer–Catanese–Pignatelli–2011].
