Birational boundedness of varieties with large Seshadri constants

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (64 pages)

Abstract: We prove that, in each dimension at least two, the normal projective varieties whose anti-canonical divisor has moving Seshadri constant at least the dimension minus one are birationally bounded. Thus we answer Question 8.6 in Zhuang’s paper on Fano varieties with large Seshadri constants.
