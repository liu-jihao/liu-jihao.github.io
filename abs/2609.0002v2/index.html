Fujita freeness in dimension six

Jihao Liu

PDF (162 pages)

Abstract: We prove that KX + 7L is globally generated for every smooth complex projective sixfold X and every ample Cartier divisor L. The proof constructs, at each point, an effective rational boundary of coefficient strictly less than seven with that point as its minimal log canonical center. It combines bounds for complete cutting chains with uniform local dimension estimates and amplification of complete spaces of sections, while retaining the full boundary and all fixed components. The main result of this paper was obtained using the Danus system.
