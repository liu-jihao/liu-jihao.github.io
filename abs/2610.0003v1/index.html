Upper bound of minimal log discrepancies

Jihao Liu

PDF (2 pages)

Abstract: We show that the minimal log discrepancy of any variety of fixed dimension has an upper bound. Our result is based on the recent result of OpenAI [OAI26, Proposition 2.1].

Comments:2 pages. Solves Problem 1.2 of [Kawakita–2011], which is Conjecture 1.4 of [Nakamura–2016].
