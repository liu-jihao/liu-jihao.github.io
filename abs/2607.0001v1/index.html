Syzygy bundles on Picard rank one varieties need not be stable

Jihao Liu

PDF (15 pages)

Abstract: We construct a smooth complex projective threefold X of Picard rank 1 and a nontrivial globally generated line bundle L on X whose syzygy bundle ML is not semistable with respect to any polarization. This answers a question of Sarkar in the negative. The main result of this paper is obtained by generative AI, particularly ChatGPT 5.5 Pro and the Rethlas system.
