Proof of the Hassett–Tschinkel weak approximation conjecture

Jihao Liu

PDF (18 pages)

Abstract: We prove the Hassett–Tschinkel conjecture that smooth geometrically rationally connected varieties over the function field of a complex curve satisfy weak approximation at all places, including places of bad reduction.

Comments:

18 pages. Proves Conjecture 2 of [Hassett–Tschinkel–2006] and Conjecture 1.1 of [Xu–2012]. We are informed by Zhiyu Tian that some other experts figured out the proof independently at the same time. The proof is simple and essentially immediate after Du–Guo–Xie, arXiv:2609.11883 (the result should be well known to experts), so the author claims no credit for the result; the paper is here as a reference for the community.
