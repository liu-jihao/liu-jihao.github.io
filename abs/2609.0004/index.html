K-polystability of nilpotent orbit and Slodowy slice bases

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (71 pages)

Abstract: We give affirmative answers to both parts of Xu–Zhuang Problem 23 in the normal/orbifold projective-base formulation, for every complex semisimple Lie algebra. For normalized nonzero nilpotent orbit closures, we prove K-polystability of the projective base of ordinary scalar dilation. For each positive-dimensional normalized irreducible component of a reduced Slodowy-slice intersection, we prove log K-polystability of the base of its effective Kazhdan action with the actual divisorial stabilizer boundary. Both conclusions include weak Kähler–Einstein currents and the equality case for all normal relatively ample test configurations. The proof constructs global homogeneous potentials through framed hyperkähler comparisons, transports them in the correct natural coordinates, and checks weighted descent and affine products. The main result of this paper was obtained using the Danus system.
