Finite degree formulas for Frobenius volumes of pairs

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (18 pages)

Abstract: In this paper we study the Frobenius volumes, an analogue of local volumes for strongly F-regular singularities over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p > 0. We prove the finite degree formula for Frobenius volumes, and show that the Hilbert–Kunz Frobenius volume equals the Frobenius volume divided by n!, where n is the dimension of the ambient variety. This gives a positive answer to Problem 49 in Xu–Zhuang’s open problem list in K-stability of Fano varieties.
