A uniform bound for log Calabi–Yau iterates of plane endomorphisms

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (119 pages)

Abstract: We prove that for the 17280 · 2200-th iterate of any polarized endomorphism of the projective plane, the pair consisting of the plane and the normalized ramification divisor is log canonical. Hence Question 6.3 of Luo–Meng has an affirmative answer, with a bound on the iterate that is explicit and independent of the degree of the endomorphism.
