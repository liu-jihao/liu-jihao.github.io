Nonhyperlinear groups exist

Jihao Liu
(The final version arXiv:2609.37761's authors are: Vadim Alekseev, Jihao Liu, and Andreas Thom. Please cite the final version.)

PDF (57 pages)

Abstract: We construct a countable discrete group that is not hyperlinear. It is a wreath product built from a polynomial elementary subgroup of an explicit Laurent semidirect product, and a specified nonidentity product of two lamps vanishes under every homomorphism into a tracial matrix ultraproduct. We prove that commutants of finitely generated Kazhdan groups are internal in the original matrix dimensions, and that an internal subgroup commutant is normalized by a Kazhdan group generated by subgroup compressions. The argument constructs expanding matrix blocks from a quotient spectral gap, matches their full multiplicity algebras, and controls both matrix sizes and multiplicities under compression. The main result of this paper was obtained using the Danus system.
