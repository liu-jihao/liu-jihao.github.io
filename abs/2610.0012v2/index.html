Quaternionic structures on conical symplectic singularities

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (73 pages)

Abstract: We prove that the coordinate ring of any conical symplectic singularity with a grading induced by the minimizer of the normalized volume admits, for any torus of graded Poisson automorphisms, a quaternionic structure commuting with the maximal compact subgroup of the torus, and hence the weights of the torus on each graded piece, counted with multiplicity, are symmetric under negation. We also prove that the identity component of the group of graded Poisson automorphisms is reductive, that twisting the action on each graded piece by any Chevalley involution yields an isomorphic representation, and that the grading descended to a Hamiltonian reduction by such a torus is again induced by the minimizer of the normalized volume if the reduction, with the descended symplectic form, is a symplectic singularity. We thereby solve Problems 27, 28, and 29 and Parts (1) and (2) of Problem 30 in Xu and Zhuang’s list of open problems on K-stability of Fano varieties.
