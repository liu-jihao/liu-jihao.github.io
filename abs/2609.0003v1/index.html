Fujita freeness on complex projective sevenfolds

Jihao Liu

PDF (319 pages)

Abstract: We prove that KX + 8L is globally generated for every smooth complex projective sevenfold X and every ample Cartier divisor L. The proof constructs complete spaces of sections with prescribed point order and controls the entire sequence of log canonical centers arising from their thresholds. Uniform bounds for the full section images, retained adjunction data, and a finite induction on intrinsic sources give the strict coefficient bound needed for point evaluation. The finite inequalities are accompanied by exact rational certificates and independently executable checkers in a documented supplement. The main result of this paper was obtained using the Danus system.
