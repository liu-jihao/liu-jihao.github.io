A generalization of Bombieri's effective birationality theorem in high dimensions

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (38 pages)

Abstract: We prove that on normal projective varieties, adjoint linear systems of nef and big Cartier divisors define birational maps with the constants of Fujita’s conjecture, and so do pluricanonical systems when the canonical divisor is Cartier, nef, and big. In particular, we solve Questions 5.10 and 5.11 of Han’s paper on Fujita’s freeness conjecture, and we prove the birationality part of Catanese’s conjecture on pluricanonical maps.
