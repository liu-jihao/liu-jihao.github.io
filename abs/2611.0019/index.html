Asymptotics of local volumes near log canonical thresholds

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (81 pages)

Abstract: We prove that, when the coefficient of a ℚ-Cartier ℚ-divisor added to the boundary of a klt singularity increases to the log canonical threshold, the local volume is asymptotic to a positive constant times the distance to the threshold raised to the codimension of the minimal log canonical center of the pair at the threshold. When the minimal log canonical center is not a point, we compute the constant in terms of the log canonical places over the center and the generalized pair induced on the center by adjunction. We also answer Question 6.10 of Li–Liu–Xu’s survey on normalized volume positively.
