The Hodge conjecture for Gaussian and Eisenstein abelian sixfolds

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (131 pages)

Abstract: We prove the Hodge conjecture for Gaussian and Eisenstein abelian sixfolds, that is, abelian sixfolds whose endomorphism algebra contains the Gaussian integers or the Eisenstein integers. In particular, the Weil classes on Gaussian and Eisenstein abelian sixfolds of Weil type are algebraic for every discriminant of the Hermitian form. We also prove the Hodge conjecture for any nonsimple abelian sixfold, except for the sixfolds isogenous to products of a fourfold and a surface of one explicit type.
