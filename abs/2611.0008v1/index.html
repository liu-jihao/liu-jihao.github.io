The fundamental group of numerical Godeaux surfaces

Jihao Liu, Wenfei Liu, and Lei Zhang

PDF (15 pages)

Abstract: We prove that the fundamental group of any numerical Godeaux surface is finite. Hence it is cyclic of order at most five and isomorphic to the torsion subgroup of the Picard group. In particular, a numerical Godeaux surface is simply connected if and only if its Picard group is torsion-free.

Comments:15 pages. Answers Question 10 of [Bauer–Catanese–Pignatelli–2011] positively when K2 = 1.
