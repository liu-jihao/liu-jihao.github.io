Dulac individual finiteness by a complete Écalle route

Jihao Liu

PDF (245 pages)

Abstract: We prove that every real planar polynomial vector field has only finitely many isolated periodic orbits. The proof follows Écalle’s formalization and actualization architecture by reconstructing complete physical return maps, including divergent normalizers and compatible nonreal transition data, and controlling the entire remainder after all cancellations. The main result of this paper was obtained using generative AI, particularly gpt-6-astra and the Danus system.

Comments:

245 pages. A proof of Dulac’s theorem that follows the two steps of Écalle’s 1992 proof, formalization and actualization, but carries them out by other methods. The OpenAI preprint [OpenAI–2026–143], made public on October 6, 2026 (UTC), gives a complete solution of the second part of Hilbert’s sixteenth problem, which implies this result; this manuscript stays here as a reference and will not be updated.
