Proof of the tropical Hodge conjecture for tropical abelian varieties

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (41 pages)

Abstract: In every ambient dimension, for every full-rank real period lattice, and in every cycle dimension, we prove that the rational classes in the kernel of the eigenwave on a flat integral affine translation torus are exactly the classes of finite signed balanced straight tropical cycles with rational coefficients. Representatives have the exact prescribed marked homology class on the original torus, with bounded rational-faced cells and finitely many cell orbits. In particular, every rational tropical Hodge class on every tropical abelian variety has such a representative. The proof uses complete endpoint moments, finite signed specialization and filling, periodic polyhedral realization, and a direct Stokes calculation of the full class. The main result of this paper was obtained using the Danus system.

Comments:

41 pages. Proves Conjecture 1.2 of [Amini–Piquerez–2020] for tropical abelian varieties. The credit for the tropical Hodge conjecture belongs to Omid Amini and Matthieu Piquerez, who proved it for rationally triangulable tropical varieties in 2020 and announced an outline of a proof of the general case in June 2024; I have not seen a written version, and their result may already contain the theorem of this paper.
