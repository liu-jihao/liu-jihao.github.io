Termination of real flips in dimension four

Jihao Liu

PDF (28 pages)

Abstract: OpenAI recently claimed a proof of the termination of flips for fourfold projective log canonical pairs with rational coefficients, but the relative case and the real coefficients case were not proved. In this paper we prove the termination of (relative) flips for all fourfold log canonical pairs, hence the termination of (relative) MMP for all fourfold log canonical pairs.
