Normalized volumes are not lower semi-continuous in valuation spaces

Jihao Liu

PDF (3 pages)

Abstract: We show that the normalized volume function on the space of valuations centered at a point is not lower semi-continuous. This disproves a hypothesis of Li, which is also listed as an open question in Zhuang’s survey.

Comments:

3 pages. Disproves Conjecture 7.1(1) of [Li–2018] and answers Question 10.6.20 of [Zhuang–2025] negatively.
