A counterexample to the Popa–Schnell global generation conjecture

Jihao Liu

PDF (6 pages)

Abstract: We provide a counterexample to the Popa–Schnell global generation conjecture. The counterexample consists of a contraction from a smooth projective threefold to a smooth projective surface.

Comments:
