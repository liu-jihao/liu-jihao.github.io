Anti-canonical moving Seshadri constants and varieties of Fano type

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (36 pages)

Abstract: We prove that a normal projective variety is of Fano type if the moving Seshadri constant of the anti-canonical divisor is at least the dimension of the variety. We thus answer Question 8.2 of Zhuang’s paper on Fano varieties with large Seshadri constants.
