Failure of ACC for minimal log discrepancies of Kollár components

Jihao Liu
(Authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.)

PDF (18 pages)

Abstract: We show that the minimal log discrepancies of Kollár components of klt threefold singularities do not satisfy the ACC. Thus we answer negatively Question 7.3 in Zhuang’s paper on boundedness of singularities and minimal log discrepancies of Kollár components, as well as the ACC part of Question 10.6.11 in his survey on stability of klt singularities.
