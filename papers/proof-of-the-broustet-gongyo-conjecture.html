We prove the Broustet–Gongyo conjecture. It asserts that any normal projective variety admitting a polarized endomorphism is of Calabi–Yau type, and we prove the conclusion more generally for normal projective varieties admitting an int-amplified endomorphism. For a polarized endomorphism of a normal projective variety, we also show that after replacing the endomorphism by an iterate, the variety together with the normalized ramification divisor of the endomorphism is a log canonical pair with Q-linearly trivial log canonical divisor, as conjectured by Chang and Zhang.
