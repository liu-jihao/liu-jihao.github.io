Inversion of stability for log canonical pairs on a fixed germ

We prove that on a fixed germ of a normal variety, if the boundary coefficients of a log canonical pair are raised to values in a fixed finite set by less than a constant depending only on the germ and the finite set, then the log canonical divisor stays ℝ-Cartier and the pair stays log canonical. Thus the conclusion of a conjecture of Han and Luo holds near the point of the germ for log canonical pairs, even if the germ is not klt. We also show that the conjecture fails for the threefold node, so the log canonical assumption cannot be removed.
