The geometric Jordan property fails for rational threefolds

We prove that the geometric Jordan property for the Cremona group, conjectured by Moraga, fails in dimension three. More precisely, we construct faithful actions of cyclic groups of arbitrarily large order on smooth rational affine threefolds such that no non-trivial subgroup is contained in an algebraic torus of birational automorphisms.
