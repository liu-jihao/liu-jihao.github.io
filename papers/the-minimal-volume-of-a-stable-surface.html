The minimal volume of a stable surface

I am working on this project in collaboration with Wenfei Liu, and the verification is in progress.

We prove that the minimal volume of klt stable surfaces is 1/48983, which is the surface case of a conjecture of Totaro. Moreover, up to isomorphism the surface of Alexeev and W. Liu is the only klt stable surface of volume 1/48983. As an application, we show that every normal stable surface has at least two linearly independent m-canonical sections for every integer m ≥ 887. We also obtain birationality of |m(KX + Δ)| for every stable surface pair (X, Δ) with reduced boundary and every integer m ≥ 1882.
