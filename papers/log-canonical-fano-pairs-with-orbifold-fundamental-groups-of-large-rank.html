Log canonical Fano pairs with orbifold fundamental groups of large rank

Status: Verified by the author.

We show that a conjecture of Gachet, Liu, and Moraga on the orbifold fundamental groups of log canonical Fano pairs fails in any dimension at least 3: There exists a log canonical Fano variety whose orbifold fundamental group is free abelian of rank strictly larger than the dimension of the variety.
