The third largest normalized volume of klt singularities

We prove that a klt singularity of dimension at least four which is neither smooth nor an ordinary double point has normalized volume at most that of the product of the affine line and the ordinary double point of dimension one less. Since smooth points and ordinary double points have the largest and the second largest normalized volumes, the normalized volume of the product is the third largest normalized volume of klt singularities in each dimension at least four.
