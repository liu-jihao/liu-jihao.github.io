Vector bundles with holomorphic connections but no flat holomorphic connections

A holomorphic vector bundle on a compact Kähler manifold which admits a holomorphic connection has vanishing real Chern classes of positive degree by a theorem of Atiyah, and it is a long-standing question whether such a bundle admits a flat holomorphic connection. We construct a holomorphic vector bundle on a smooth projective surface which admits a holomorphic connection but no flat holomorphic connection, answering the question negatively. The associated projective bundle disproves a conjecture formulated by Höring on compact Kähler manifolds with split tangent bundle, in which the summands are not assumed to be integrable, and shows that the integrability assumption in Beauville’s conjecture on such manifolds cannot be dropped even if one asks only for a product decomposition of the universal cover into factors whose dimensions are the ranks of the summands.
