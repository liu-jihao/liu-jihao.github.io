Optimal upper bound of volumes for varieties with large Seshadri constants

We prove that if the anti-canonical divisor of a normal projective variety has moving Seshadri constant greater than the dimension minus one, then the product of the anti-canonical volume and the excess of the moving Seshadri constant over the dimension minus one is at most twice the anti-canonical volume of the projective space of equal dimension, with equality for the projective space. In particular, the anti-canonical volume is at most twice Euler’s number times the dimension to the power of the dimension, divided by the excess of the moving Seshadri constant over the dimension minus one, and the constant twice Euler’s number is optimal. We thus answer Question 8.1 of Zhuang’s paper on Fano varieties with large Seshadri constants in the affirmative.
