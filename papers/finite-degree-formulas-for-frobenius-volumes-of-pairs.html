In this paper we study the Frobenius volumes, an analogue of local volumes for strongly F-regular singularities over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p > 0. We prove the finite degree formula for Frobenius volumes, and show that the Hilbert–Kunz Frobenius volume equals the Frobenius volume divided by n!, where n is the dimension of the ambient variety. This gives a positive answer to Problem 49 in Xu–Zhuang’s open problem list in K-stability of Fano varieties.
