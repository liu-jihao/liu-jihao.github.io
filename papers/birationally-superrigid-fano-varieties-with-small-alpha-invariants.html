Birationally superrigid Fano varieties with small alpha invariants

We prove that there exist birationally superrigid Fano varieties with arbitrarily small alpha invariants, which provides another negative answer to a question of Stibitz and Zhuang whether the alpha invariant of a birationally superrigid Fano variety is always greater than 1/2. Our examples are hypersurfaces in weighted projective spaces, among them one of dimension 15 whose alpha invariant is at most 1/3. We also prove that a smooth quadric threefold admits a faithful action of a finite group of order 7200 such that the quadric is equivariantly birationally superrigid and has equivariant alpha invariant at most 1/3, which answers an equivariant question of Cheltsov, Sarikyan, and Zhuang in the negative in dimension three.
