We prove that all smooth prime Fano threefolds of genus ten (family 1.9) are K-stable and that all smooth prime Fano threefolds of genus twelve (family 1.10) are K-semistable, and thereby solve Problem 10 of Xu–Zhuang’s open problem list on K-stability of Fano varieties, which asks to show that any smooth Fano threefold of Picard number one is K-semistable. For smooth prime Fano threefolds of genus twelve, we show that K-stability is equivalent to each special line intersecting another line and to the GIT-stability of the Hilbert scheme of lines as a plane quartic, that K-polystability is equivalent to the GIT-polystability of the quartic, and that each K-polystable one is isomorphic to a member of an explicit family over an open set of binary octics whose fiber over zero is the Mukai–Umemura threefold. We also disprove the original formulation of Donaldson’s conjecture, which predicts that such a threefold without a torus action is not K-polystable as soon as an anti-canonical divisor has a specific simple elliptic singularity, by an explicit K-stable example with finite automorphism group and with such a divisor, and we prove the prediction when the singular point is contained in a line.
