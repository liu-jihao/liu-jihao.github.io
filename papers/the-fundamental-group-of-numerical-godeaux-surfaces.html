The fundamental group of numerical Godeaux surfaces

This paper and no. 41, Classification of numerical Godeaux surfaces, are companion papers. I am working on this project in collaboration with Wenfei Liu and Lei Zhang, and the verification is in progress.

We prove that the fundamental group of any numerical Godeaux surface is finite. Hence it is cyclic of order at most five and isomorphic to the torsion subgroup of the Picard group. In particular, a numerical Godeaux surface is simply connected if and only if its Picard group is torsion-free.
