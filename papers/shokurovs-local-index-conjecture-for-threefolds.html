We prove Shokurov’s local index conjecture in dimension three: for every positive real number a there is a positive integer R3(a) such that, for every klt threefold germ X ∋ x with mld(X ∋ x) = a, the divisor R3(a)KX is Cartier near x. More generally, for every finite set Γ of rational numbers in [0,1] there is a positive integer R3(a,Γ) such that R3(a,Γ)(KX + B) is Cartier near x for every klt threefold germ (X ∋ x, B) with the coefficients of B in Γ and mld(X ∋ x, B) = a.
