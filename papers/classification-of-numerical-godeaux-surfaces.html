This paper and no. 42, The fundamental group of numerical Godeaux surfaces, are companion papers. I am working on this project in collaboration with Wenfei Liu and Lei Zhang, and the verification is in progress.

We prove Reid’s conjecture that the moduli space of numerical Godeaux surfaces has exactly five irreducible components, one for each cyclic fundamental group of order at most 5. In particular, there exist exactly five deformation classes of numerical Godeaux surfaces, and any numerical Godeaux surface with torsion-free Picard group is deformation equivalent to the Barlow surface.
