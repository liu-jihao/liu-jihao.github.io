Campana’s abelianity conjecture for projective orbifolds

We prove that the orbifold fundamental group of a special smooth projective orbifold pair is virtually abelian. This answers a conjecture of Campana and extends a recent result of OpenAI to the orbifold projective case.

Relation to the OpenAI manuscripts. Campana conjectured in 2011 that the orbifold fundamental group of a special orbifold pair is virtually abelian. The OpenAI manuscript The abelianity conjecture for special compact Kähler manifolds (made public on October 6, 2026, UTC) proves the case of compact Kähler manifolds, and the OpenAI manuscript A conditional abelianity theorem for special fourfold pairs with a half-weight divisor (made public on the same day) treats the case of a smooth projective fourfold with one boundary component of multiplicity two. This paper proves the projective orbifold case in general, with arbitrary multiplicities and several boundary components. The proof uses, without reproving them, Theorem 1.1 of the first manuscript and Lemmas 2.1, 2.2 and 2.4 of the second; the main theorem is therefore conditional on those results, which have not been independently verified.
