This page lists the papers whose first versions were produced with AI systems, and the notes posted on this website.

In principle, papers posted on arXiv on or before September 23, 2026 are not listed here; the one exception is liuxiv:2608.0001, The equality case of Ehrhart’s volume conjecture.

Each paper has a permanent identifier liuxiv:YYMM.NNNN, where YYMM is the year and month of its first posting and NNNN its order within that month. Its abstract page is jihaoliu.org/abs/YYMM.NNNN/.

About “AI-generated”. On this page, “AI-generated” describes how the first version of a paper was produced: an AI system wrote it, working from the problem, ideas and prompts that humans provided. Even the first version is therefore not purely AI-generated, and later versions may have been revised by humans, verified and revised by humans, or edited for writing by humans.

Machine-verified (✓): the proof has been checked by an internal AI verifier and by AI reviews of the manuscript; this is not formal verification. Human-verified (✓): the proof has been verified by a human. ✗ means that the paper’s entry says this has not been done; – means that it says nothing about it. Readers should independently check every mathematical claim before relying on or citing it.

Authors marked with *. An asterisk (*) after the authors of a paper means that the authorship is temporary and only for citation before the final version of the paper. Collaboration on human verification, understanding, and polishing the writing of these papers is very welcome. If you are interested in collaboration, please contact Jihao Liu directly.
